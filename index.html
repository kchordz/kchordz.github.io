02 / ABOUT ME

Creative vision.
Practical craft.

I’m Kenneth Egbuchue, a Lagos-based creative with over 11 years of experience across graphic design, brand management and print production.

My work connects concept with execution—from the first sketch and visual direction to the finished digital asset or printed piece.

I work with Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and CorelDRAW, bringing attention to typography, composition and production detail.

Brand identity

Logos, visual systems and brand materials that give businesses a consistent presence.

Campaign design

Advertising visuals, social content and launch assets built around a clear creative idea.

Print production

Print-ready artwork, large-format graphics and branded materials with careful finishing.
